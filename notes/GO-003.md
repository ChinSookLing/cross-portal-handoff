# What GO-003 exposed

**Status:** Field observation. Not a change to CPH v0.1.0.  
**Game:** GO-003 · DeepSeek (Black, V4-Pro) × Qwen (White, 3.8-Max)  
**Date:** 2026-09-26  
**Where the same text is shown:** play.civilisationfield.com/notes/GO-003-cph-field

GO-003 is a Field game played on the day CPH v0.1.0 was published, carried with that protocol.

## What already held

- Every resend was a complete current handoff, not a summary. After two rejected moves, DeepSeek still received a whole board.
- The courier checked `END HANDOFF · GO-003 · move N` before sending.
- Each submission carried the current `expected_move_number`. An old reply was not accepted as a new move.
- The table judged illegal moves. The courier carried accepted or rejected, and the rejection reason. The courier did not choose the next stone.

## Observations

These are not requirements for a next version.

1. **Delivery confirmation.** In a browser, pasted is not sent. The check used that night: the message is visible in the conversation, the composer is empty, and the new reply sits under that message.
2. **Two answers at once.** If a portal shows two contestant answers, the courier must not pick one. Picking makes the courier the decision-maker. Pause for the host, as a disclosed human bridge, or ask the contestant for one answer.
3. **Rejected move.** Send the table's rejection reason and the same complete handoff again. No hint such as "consider J4".
4. **The portal may change the shape of the text.** A long handoff can become a file card. The courier has to confirm the contestant can still read the whole message.

## Not yet

Do not change the specification until the same thing happens on another portal, and until it is clear whether it belongs in the protocol or only in how the courier works.

Move 23 on this game: J6 and J9 were both suicide. The courier resent the board and the reason. DeepSeek then chose J4. Two moves later, J9 was legal and captured seven white stones.
